# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the Konspec UI community to be welcoming, professional, and safe for everyone. Participation is expected to be free from harassment and discrimination, regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or status.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and changes on their merits.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and the time of other contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised conduct, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person’s private information without permission are not acceptable. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, other project spaces, and public interactions while representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub’s **Report content** feature or to a maintainer listed on the [konfpa GitHub organisation](https://github.com/konfpa). Reports will be handled promptly, fairly, and with appropriate regard for privacy.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a private warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant when necessary to protect the community. Decisions will be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.
