A skipped task is not an unfinished one

  • It leaves the denominator, so 9 of 15 rather than 9 of 17, and both figures stay on the page.
  • It never appears in what is next, and it never appears in the work-left estimate.
  • Anything waiting on it waits for ever. A skipped task never finishes, so the dependency never clears — that is the one cost, and it is why the screen names what is stranded.
  • It can be brought back at any time, which puts it into the denominator again and the count down with it.
