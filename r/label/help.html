Eight digits. It sets the GST rate on every line raised against this item.

HSN is the customs classification the GST rate hangs off. The first two digits are the chapter, the next two the heading and the last four the tariff item, so 3901 20 00 is polyethylene of specific gravity 0.94 or more.

A wrong code is not a display fault. It changes the tax on every invoice raised against the item, and correcting it afterwards means a credit note for each one.
