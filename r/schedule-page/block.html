The scheduled block

One lane, 06:00 to 22:00. Every block below is the same record at a different duration.

What the text does

  • A fortnight, or the whole lane

    MS-4838 06:00 – 22:00 · 16.0 h Injection 120T · 9,500 crates · Meera Joshi

    Number, times, duration, resource, quantity, owner. Everything the record has that a planner reads at a glance.

  • Half the lane — eight hours

    MS-4838 06:00 – 14:00 · 9,500 crates Injection 120T, Meera Joshi

    The owner and the resource drop out of the block; the lane already names the resource and the record page holds the rest.

  • Two hours

    4838 MS-4838, Injection 120T, 06:00 to 08:00, 9,500 crates, Meera Joshi

    The prefix goes before the digits do. MS- is the same on every block on the screen; 4838 is the one part that identifies this one.

  • Forty minutes

    The dot and nothing else, with the whole record in the accessible name. Widening it to fit the number would draw forty minutes as two hours, which is the one thing this layout may not do.

What the edges do

  • Started before this lane began

    MS-4835 Started 21:00 yesterday · ends 11:30

    Square on the left, rounded on the right, and the start date written in. A rounded left edge at 06:00 says the job started at 06:00.

  • Runs past the end of the shift

    MS-4838 14:00 – 23:30 · runs 1.5 h past shift end

    The overrun is in the text as well as in the clip, and the lane it sits on carries the amber mark on its booked-of-available figure.

  • Two on one lane at one time

    MS-4835 · 06:00 – 14:00 MS-4841 · 12:00 – 20:00

    The lane grows and the two blocks keep their true left and width, so the overlap is a shape. Squeezing both into one row hides the two hours that are the whole problem.
