Annexure B — commercial terms

RC-26-0041 · Gujarat Polymers Ltd · 04 Apr 2026

  1. 4.

    Delivery and freight

    1. 4.1 Material is delivered ex-works Nashik. Freight is billed at actuals against the transporter's receipt and does not carry the contract discount.
    2. 4.2 A delivery window of 14 days from the order date applies to any quantity up to 20 MT.
    3. 4.10 Short receipts beyond 2% of the ordered quantity are debited at the contract rate without further intimation.
  2. 5.

    Payment

    1. 5.1 45 days from the date of the goods receipt note, against a tax invoice quoting the order number.
    2. 5.2 Interest of 1% a month runs on any amount held beyond the credit period and is set off against the next invoice.
