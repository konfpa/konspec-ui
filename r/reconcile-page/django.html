Working that out in the template means a query inside a loop and a page that is fine on the demo data and unusable on a month of it. reconciliation_totals() returns invoiced, received and difference over the whole invoice and the same three over the exception lines only, so the band at the top and the foot of the table cannot disagree about which population they describe. {% endcomment %}
