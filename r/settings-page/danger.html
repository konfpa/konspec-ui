Deactivate and reset

Three acts that cannot be reversed from this screen. Each one is separate and each one is guarded on its own.

Deactivate Vapi plant

Stops new orders, GRNs and issues being raised against VAPI-02. The 34 open orders, 61 unpaid invoices and ₹27,10,400 of committed value stay in the ledgers and stay readable. The plant is never deleted — a plant with ledger history against it cannot be.

Reset the GRN series

Sets the running number back to 1 for FY 2026–27. GRN-4821 and everything before it keeps its number, so the register will hold two receipts numbered GRN-0001 once the first new one is posted.

Revoke every API key

4 keys are in use, the oldest issued 11 Feb 2025. The weighbridge feed and the Tally export both authenticate with one of them and stop within the minute.

Every act on this page is written to the audit trail with your name against it.

Deactivate Vapi plant

9 users work only at this plant and lose the ability to raise anything from tomorrow morning. Reactivating is a support request, not a button.
