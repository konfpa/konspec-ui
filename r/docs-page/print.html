Purpose and scope

Booking a receipt of purchased material at the gate, against an approved purchase order, at either plant. It does not cover receipts against a subcontracting challan, which is SOP-STO-005.

Procedure

  1. 1

    Check the challan against the order

    The challan carries the order number. If it does not, or it carries one that is closed, the vehicle does not come off the weighbridge until the buyer confirms the order.

  2. 2

    Open the order in Receipts

    Stores → Receipts → Against an order, or scan the order barcode on the challan.

  3. 3

    Enter the vehicle and the challan

    Vehicle number, challan number and date, and the e-way bill where the taxable value is above ₹50,000.

  4. 4

    Weigh in and record the tare

    The net is gross less tare and is computed. Do not type a net weight over it.

    A difference over 0.5% against the challan needs a shortage note before the GRN posts. Do not adjust the weights to make the difference go away.

  5. 5

    Enter the received quantity per line

    Count what is on the vehicle, not what the challan says.

  6. 6

    Post the GRN

    The number is allotted on posting and cannot be reused. A GRN posted in error is reversed, not deleted.
