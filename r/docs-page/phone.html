Raising a capital indent

SOP-PUR-004 · Revision 3 · Effective 01 Apr 2026

A capital indent commits money that is not in the plant's operating budget, so it takes a budget code before it takes a vendor.

On this page
  • When an indent is capital
  • Raising it
  • Who approves it

When an indent is capital

An indent is capital when what is bought is expected to last more than one financial year and is carried on the fixed asset register. The test is the asset register and not the value.

  • Plant and machinery, including a rebuild that extends the life of a machine.
  • Moulds, dies and tooling held against a part number.
  • Anything installed in the building.

Raising it

Open Materials → Indents → New and choose Capital. The type cannot be changed after the indent is saved.

A capital indent without a budget code will not route to anybody.

It saves as a draft and sits there.

Who approves it

The indent routes on its value, and the value is the whole indent rather than the line.

Approval limits for a capital indent, by value
Value Approver Needs
To ₹2,00,000 Plant head —
To ₹10,00,000 Plant head, Finance 2 quotes
Above ₹10,00,000 Director 3 quotes
