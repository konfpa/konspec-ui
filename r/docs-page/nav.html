An order is amended when a term on it changes after it has been approved — the quantity, the rate, the delivery date or the freight. Every amendment raises the revision number and reissues the document to the vendor.

What may be amended

Anything except the vendor and the plant. Changing either of those is a new order, because the first one has already been sent to a vendor who may have started against it.

  • Quantity, up or down, on a line that has not been fully received.
  • Rate, which re-routes the order for approval if the new value crosses a limit.
  • Delivery date, which is the amendment that does not need re-approval.
