Booking a goods receipt

SOP-STO-002 · Revision 7 · Effective 01 Jul 2026 · Owner: Stores, Silvassa

Nine steps, about fifteen minutes for a single-vehicle receipt. Do them at the gate, in this order, before the vehicle is released — a GRN booked after the lorry has gone cannot be corrected against the weighbridge.

Before you start

  • The vehicle is on the weighbridge and the gross weight is recorded.
  • You have the vendor challan and, for a taxable supply, the e-way bill number.
  • The purchase order is approved and not closed.
  1. 1

    Check the challan against the order

    The challan carries the order number. If it does not, or it carries one that is closed, the vehicle does not come off the weighbridge until the buyer confirms which order it is against.

  2. 2

    Open the order in Receipts

    Stores → Receipts → Against an order. The screen scans a barcode as well, which is faster at the gate and is the same form either way.

    Open the receipt screen

  3. 3

    Enter the vehicle and the challan

    Vehicle number, challan number, challan date and the e-way bill. The e-way bill is required above ₹50,000 of taxable value and the form will not post without it.

  4. 4

    Weigh in and record the tare

    The net is gross less tare and the system computes it. Do not type a net weight over it: the two figures are what the vendor is paid on and a typed net that does not reconcile is found at the monthly weighbridge audit, by which time the vehicle is four weeks gone.

    A difference over 0.5% against the challan needs a shortage note before the GRN posts.

    Step 7 covers it. Do not adjust the weights to make the difference go away.

  5. 5

    Enter the received quantity per line

    Count what is on the vehicle, not what the challan says. The two columns sit beside each other on the screen for that reason.

  6. 6

    Post the GRN

    The number is allotted on posting — GRN-4821 — and it cannot be reused. A GRN posted in error is reversed and a new one raised; there is no delete.

  7. 7

    Raise a shortage note if there is a difference

    Short, excess or damaged, on the GRN itself, while the vehicle is still on site. The driver signs the shortage note and the copy travels back with the vehicle.

  8. 8

    Put the material away against the bin

    Material against an inspection plan goes to the quarantine bay and stays on inward hold until Quality passes it. See SOP-QA-011, QC hold and release.

  9. 9

    Release the vehicle

    Gate out against the GRN number. The gate register and the GRN reconcile nightly and a vehicle released without one is the exception the security supervisor is asked about the next morning.
