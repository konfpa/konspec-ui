QC hold and release

SOP-QA-011 · Revision 5 · Effective 01 Jul 2026

A hold stops material moving. It is not a rejection and it is not a note on the record — while a lot is on hold nobody may issue it, and the store cannot override that from the issue screen.

What a hold does

Placing a hold writes a block against the lot in the batch ledger. The lot keeps its quantity and its location, so it is still counted in stock and still valued, and every issue against it fails with the hold reference rather than with a generic error.

A hold is placed by Quality and by nobody else. A buyer who believes material is wrong raises a re-inspection request against the lot, which is what puts it in front of Quality.

Three kinds of hold

The kind is chosen when the hold is placed and decides who may release it:

Inward hold
Placed automatically on every lot received against a material with an inspection plan. Released by the inspector who passes the lot.
Quality hold
Placed by an inspector on a lot already in stock, usually after a complaint from the line. Released by the QA head.
Customer hold
Placed against finished goods when a customer has raised a claim. Released by the QA head with the claim reference on the record.

Releasing a hold

A release needs a result on the record. Not a phone call, not an email to the store — the lab result or the inspection has to be entered against the lot first, because the release records which result it was made on and that link is what an audit follows.

  1. Open the lot in Quality → Holds.
  2. Check that the result you are releasing against is listed under the hold.
  3. Choose Release and enter the reason.
  4. The block clears on the next issue attempt, which is immediate.

A hold that has to stay while part of the lot is used is not a partial release. Split the lot first — Quality → Holds → Split — and release the child lot. Releasing and re-holding leaves a gap in the ledger during which the whole lot was issuable.

If the material has already moved

Material issued before a hold was placed is traced through the batch ledger and recalled by the QA head. That is a different procedure and it is SOP-QA-014, Recall of issued material.

Material that does not conform to specified requirements shall be identified and controlled to prevent its unintended use or delivery.
