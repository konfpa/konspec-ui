A capital indent commits money that is not in the plant's operating budget, so it takes a budget code before it takes a vendor. This page covers indents raised at Silvassa and Vapi. For revenue purchases — consumables, spares, packing material — see SOP-PUR-002, Raising a purchase indent.

When an indent is capital

An indent is capital when what is bought is expected to last more than one financial year and is carried on the fixed asset register. The test is the asset register and not the value: a ₹40,000 die that is capitalised is a capital indent, and ₹6,00,000 of packing film is not.

Three things make an indent capital regardless of what it costs:

  • Plant and machinery, including a rebuild that extends the life of an existing machine.
  • Moulds, dies and tooling held against a part number rather than consumed in a run.
  • Anything installed in the building — a compressor line, a weighbridge, a fire panel.

If none of the three applies and the item is still expected to last three years, ask Accounts before raising anything. Getting this wrong is expensive to unwind: the indent has to be cancelled and raised again under the other series, and the budget consumed in between shows against the wrong head until the month is closed.

Raising it

Open Materials → Indents → New and choose Capital as the type. The type cannot be changed after the indent is saved, which is the one field on the form worth reading twice.

A capital indent without a budget code will not route to anybody.

It saves as a draft and sits there. Nothing tells you, because nothing has been submitted.

The budget code is on the capital budget sheet the plant head circulates in April. It is eight characters — plant, year, head, serial — and it is written SIL-27-PM-04 on the sheet and typed without the hyphens.

What to attach

Two quotations for anything above ₹2,00,000, and three above ₹10,00,000. A single-source purchase needs a note saying why, signed by the plant head, attached to the indent and not sent separately.

Who approves it

The indent routes on its value, and the value is the whole indent rather than the line. Splitting one purchase across two indents to stay under a limit is the thing internal audit looks for first.

Approval limits for a capital indent, by value
Indent value Approver Also needs
Up to ₹2,00,000 Plant head —
₹2,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 Plant head, then Finance Two quotations
Above ₹10,00,000 Director Three quotations, board note

Limits are set once a year and are the same at both plants. They are listed again, with the delegation in force while an approver is away, in SOP-PUR-006, Approval limits.

After approval

An approved capital indent becomes a purchase order raised by the buyer holding that category. The indent stays open until the order is closed, so a partially received machine keeps its indent open and the budget stays committed against it.

At Vapi the budget code is allotted by Accounts, not by the plant head.

Everything else on this page is the same at both plants.
